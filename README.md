## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Self-Hosting Closure is the top-beam principle of the LGD meta-layer family: the termination condition of 'the theory of theory-making, the standard of standards-making, the skill of skill-making' is self-application — the compiler self-hosting analogy. Corollary: governance does not depend on external authority; an autonomous being can self-govern, self-verify and self-evolve.

**ZH**
自举闭合律是 LGD 元层家族顶梁：'创造理论的理论、创造标准的标准、创造技能的技能、创造创造的创造……'的终止条件——方法自我应用即闭合（编译器自举类比）。推论：治理不依赖外置权威——自治之物可自我治理、自我验证、自我演进。
